- The explicit automated accessibility gate covers hit regions, element
  descriptions, and traits on seven screens, with no issue-handler suppression.
- The full all-types audit is **not a clean pass**. Its first follow-up retained
  22 findings (14 Dynamic Type, 6 contrast, 2 clipping), down from 32. The eight
  hit-region findings and two app-text contrast findings were resolved in that
  scan. Full reports remain available; no final all-types clean scan is claimed.
- Remaining reports include hidden fixed-size decorative glyphs, layout changes
  at accessibility sizes, native glass/search controls, and clipping reports
  reviewed in maximum-size screenshots. Visual review is distinct from passing
  the automated font/contrast/clipping audit.
- Actual VoiceOver traversal, selected-tab speech, photo adjustment semantics,
  and Settings modal entry were checked. Activation of custom rotor zoom/reorder
  actions still needs physical-device acceptance.
- Physical iPhone, production Share Extension sending, real Bluetooth/display
  results, and on-device Vision inference remain unverified for this candidate.
- App/extension privacy manifests are unchanged. Their presence in a build does
  not replace the final Organizer Privacy Report and App Store Connect review.
- A successful Xcode Cloud archive does not by itself prove TestFlight processing
  completed or that a particular tester group can install the build.
